# Privacy Policy / Política de privacidad

**yt-auto** is a personal, non-commercial automation that creates videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

- The app only requests the `youtube.upload` permission for the owner's own account.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person.
- The OAuth token is stored only as an encrypted GitHub Actions secret in the owner's private settings.
- No data is shared with third parties other than Google/YouTube APIs, which are used to upload the owner's videos.
